See an Orchestra in Concert

  • 2025-06-02
  • One-minute read

Given my musical background, I’ve seen many an orchestra concert - and indeed even played in a couple myself!

One of the standout concerts for me though, was seeing my favourite band Alter Bridge at one of two special performances at the Royal Albert Hall with the Parallax Orchestra. The performances were recorded for a special live album and DVD release.
